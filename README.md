A make.fun bounty entry. The program writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: a warning to my past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered.

## Entry

> Don't eat the gas station sushi at 2 a.m. the night before your sister's wedding.
> You missed her vows from the bathroom, and she still tells that story at every holiday dinner.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
